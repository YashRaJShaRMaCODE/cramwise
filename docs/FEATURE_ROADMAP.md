# Cramwise feature roadmap

**Status:** proposal only; none of these roadmap items are claimed as implemented unless marked shipped. Sequence is gated by user evidence. Cramwise should remain a useful private, browser-local planner instead of chasing feature parity with large study suites.

| # | Feature | User problem solved | Competitor weakness / opening | Impact | Effort | Gate / acceptance evidence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guided first-session setup | New learners need to replace sample data and reach a useful plan quickly. | MyStudyLife reviews cite setup friction; simple task apps still need study context. | High | M | 5 first-time learners create a realistic plan unassisted; keyboard and mobile checks pass. |
| 2 | CSV syllabus import with preview | Typing a long syllabus topic by topic is a barrier. | MyStudyLife offers schedule scan as a paid feature; reviews mention manual setup burden. | High | M | Import preview, validation, duplicate handling, cancel/undo, and malicious CSV safeguards tested. |
| 3 | Explainable plan reasons | Learners need to know why a topic was suggested and adjust the plan. | Broad planners offer flexible task management but not Cramwise's exam/topic-specific rationale. | High | S | Each suggestion shows priority/time-budget reason; user can override without data loss. |
| 4 | Adaptive time estimates | Initial time guesses are often inaccurate. | Reviewers of study apps ask for more adaptive revision timing. | High | M | Optional estimate suggestions use only local completed sessions; learner confirms all changes. |
| 5 | Recall confidence and review spacing | A checked-off topic may not be remembered later. | StudySmarter reviewers ask for more recall choices/adaptation; Cramwise currently tracks completion, not recall. | High | M | Learner can rate recall, see a review date, edit it, and export it; no grade or memory guarantee. |
| 6 | Topic prerequisites | Learners may plan advanced topics before prerequisites. | General task tools don't model syllabus dependencies. | High | M | Optional dependency links influence suggestions and show a cycle-free explanation. |
| 7 | Sustainable weekly capacity | A daily target can be unrealistic around classes/work/rest. | Generic planners optimize task dates, not a learner's study capacity. | High | M | Learner sets available days/minutes and rest days; overload warning never shames or auto-books time. |
| 8 | Calendar export (ICS) | Learners want a schedule in their existing calendar. | Reviews cite calendar integration limitations in general planners. | Medium | M | Local .ics export, correct timezone/date behavior, no calendar permission or background sync. |
| 9 | Printable weekly revision sheet | Some learners need paper or a shareable plan. | App-first competitors can make offline print less direct. | Medium | S | Print layout passes grayscale, pagination, and accessibility checks; export contains only selected fields. |
| 10 | Offline installable app shell | Learners may have unreliable connections or want app-like launch. | Cloud-synced competitors depend on cross-device accounts; local-first can keep core offline. | Medium | M | PWA install and offline reload verified; cache contains no private study data; upgrade invalidation tested. |
| 11 | Backup reminders and storage health | Browser data can be lost when device/browser storage is cleared or full. | Cramwise has manual JSON backup; make its limits visible and recovery easy. | High | S | Opt-in local reminder, storage warning, backup timestamp, and restore preview work without uploading data. **Restore preview shipped locally in commit `1e60cc6`.** |
| 12 | Progress insights beyond streak | Learners need to see what they practiced, not just streak count. | Broad tools provide generic reporting; an exam-oriented view can explain topic coverage. | Medium | M | Local charts/list expose totals and incomplete coverage; screen-reader alternatives and CSV export included. |
| 13 | Reusable exam templates | Repeated exam structures take time to enter. | Large learning suites have community content; Cramwise can begin with verified user-owned templates. | Medium | M | Templates include source, date, license, editable topic list, and no scraped copyrighted content. |
| 14 | Consent-based tutor review link | Some learners want support without sharing private notes. | Current student planners have family/tutor features, but reviews show setup/account complexity. | Medium | L | Only build after a signed pilot: explicit per-field consent, revocation, least-privilege access, audit and deletion tested. |
| 15 | Coaching-provider branded pilot kit | A coach needs a repeatable onboarding and plan template to test a paid service. | Existing B2C products do not prove an affordable small-coach workflow. | Medium | M | Human-run pilot with written scope, measured delivery time, buyer sign-off, and paid commitment before SaaS build. |

## Prioritization rule

Build #1–#7 only after learner interviews confirm the pain. Ship privacy/reliability before growth mechanics. Do not start #14 or account/billing work until the buyer and data-sharing model are approved. Each feature needs acceptance tests, responsive/error/empty states, and a privacy review; remove ideas that interviewees do not value.
